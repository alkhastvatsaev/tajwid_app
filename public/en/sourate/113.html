Practice Sūrat al-Falaq

Falaq is second in the boot after Ikhlāṣ. Another qul, then refuge vocabulary that many rush. Tilmidh forces word-by-word so aʿūdhu, bi-rabbi, al-falaq each earn a green box.

قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلْفَلَقِ

Use this surah to stabilize qalqalah after Ikhlāṣ and before Nās. Rules: qalqalah, madd. Positioning for beginners who pray: best tajwid app for beginners.
