  1. Practice Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ

    Written by Alkhast Vatsaev · Updated 2026-08-15

    Ikhlāṣ is short and dense with qalqalah — a good drill after Fātiḥah. Recite qul, wait until the word is accepted, continue through aḥad, Allāh, aṣ-ṣamad. Four verses are short enough to repeat three times in a row without fatigue, which is exactly what installs the bounce of the qāf.

    Verse by verse

    قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ

    Verse 1: qalqalah ṣughrā on the qāf of qul; do not swallow huwa; the lām of Allāhu is heavy after a ḍamma; the dāl of aḥad bounces if you stop. Verse 2, Allāhu ṣ-ṣamad: an emphatic ṣād, then a dāl in waqf. Verse 3, lam yalid wa-lam yūlad: clear nūn (iẓhār) and a bouncing dāl at the stop. Verse 4, wa-lam yakun lahu kufuwan aḥad: ikhfāʾ or iẓhār depending on the link, then a final qalqalah kubrā if you stop.

    What each verse trains

    VerseWhat you train

    2 — Allāhu ṣ-ṣamademphatic ṣād, dāl in waqf
    3 — lam yalid wa-lam yūladiẓhār, bouncing dāl

    The most common mistake

    Saying kul instead of qul, or stretching aḥad into a melody. The mic waits for the word, not for singing. Recite slowly: word accepted, next word. Pair this with the qalqalah guide when the bounce still feels uncertain.

    Open the mic on Ikhlāṣ

    Where to go next

    Beginners who already pray often start here, then move to Fātiḥah — see best tajwid app for beginners. After Ikhlāṣ, chain Al-Falaq and An-Nās, then return to Al-Fātiḥah.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which qalqalah appears in Al-Ikhlāṣ?

    A qalqalah ṣughrā on the qāf of qul in verse 1, and qalqalah kubrā on the final dāl of aḥad, ṣamad and yūlad whenever you stop on them.

    Is the lām of Allāh heavy in this surah?

    Yes, in Allāhu aḥad and Allāhu ṣ-ṣamad, because it follows a ḍamma. It would be light after a kasra, as in the basmala.

    Why start with Al-Ikhlāṣ?

    Because it is short and dense: four verses are enough to meet qalqalah, tafkhīm and waqf. You can repeat it several times in a row, which builds the gesture faster than a long surah.
