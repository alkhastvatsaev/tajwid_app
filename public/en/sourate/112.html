Practice Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ

Ikhlāṣ is the first pedagogical surah in Tilmidh’s boot. Short. Dense with qalqalah. You learn the mic loop before Fātiḥah. Recite qul, wait for green, continue through aḥad, Allāh, al-ṣamad.

قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ

Focus on qalqalah ṣughrā in qul and waqf bounce on aḥad if you stop. Pair with the qalqalah guide. Beginners who already pray often start here, then Fātiḥah — see best tajwid app for beginners.
