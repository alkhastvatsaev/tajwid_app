  1. Recite Yā-Sīn word by word

    Written by Alkhast Vatsaev · Updated 2026-08-15

    Yā-Sīn is the thirty-sixth surah, eighty-three verses, revealed in Makkah. It is one of the most-read surahs — for the sick, for the dead, or simply because people learned it young. Eighty-three verses is longer than Mulk, shorter than Kahf: long enough for fatigue, compact enough for one session if you split it in three.

    يسٓ

    The isolated letters. Yā-Sīn is not a word you swallow. It is a madd lāzim ḥarfī: six ḥarakāt on the sīn, not two, not “fairly long”. That is the first test of the surah, and the one almost everyone fails by rushing into wa l-qurʾāni l-ḥakīm. Hold the six counts. Only then go to verse 2.

    وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ

    Wa l-qurʾāni l-ḥakīm. Qalqalah on qāf if you stop on qurʾān in isolation; here you continue. The hamza in qurʾān must be heard — it is not qurān. The ḥāʾ of ḥakīm is not an English h.

    Three blocks, not eighty-three verses in one run

    BlockVersesWhat you train
    Opening1–12madd lāzim of يس, qalqalah, hamza of qurʾān
    The messengers13–32idghām, ghunnah, do not melt the names
    Signs and ending33–83pause madd, ikhfāʾ, same tempo to the end

    The trap in Yā-Sīn is not one verse: it is the rhythm. The rhyme in -ūn / -īn / -īm pushes you to speed up, and every speed-up shortens a pause madd. By verse 40 you no longer have the length you had at verse 2. That is what the mic makes visible: you stall on a word you thought you knew, because you swallowed it.

    The rules that carry the surah

    Madd is the subject of Yā-Sīn, from the first glyph to the verse endings. Qalqalah returns on qāf and dāl. Ghunnah on the doubled nūn of the rhyme. Idghām in the narrative joins. First work يس ten times alone, the way you would work aḍ-ḍāllīn at the end of Fātiḥah: same gesture, six ḥarakāt, before the rest of the text.

    Tilmidh shows Quran.com Uthmani tajweed, phonetics under each word, and only advances if the mic recognised the word. It does not time your six ḥarakāt on يس. It stops you skipping them on the way to verse 2. Exact duration: a muqriʾ.

    Open the mic on Yā-Sīn

    After Yā-Sīn, Al-Kahf (longer, same block logic) or Al-Mulk (shorter, evening recitation). Do not mix the three in one session.

    Frequently asked questions

    How long is يس held?

    Six ḥarakāt, madd lāzim ḥarfī. Not two. Same family as aḍ-ḍāllīn at the end of Al-Fātiḥah. If you hold them too short, the word is no longer the word.

    Why do people stall in the middle of Yā-Sīn?

    Because the rhyme gives momentum, and momentum shortens pause madd. Split into three blocks. Repeat the last two verses of the previous block each session.

    Does Tilmidh replace reciting Yā-Sīn for the dead?

    No. It is a pronunciation trainer. Devotional uses of the surah are not the subject of this page, and the app is not an ijazah.
