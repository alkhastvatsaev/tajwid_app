Practice madd

Madd is lengthening a letter of madd (ا و ي) when the conditions are met. Natural madd (aṣlī) is about two ḥarakāt. Connected or separate madd, madd lāzim, and others stretch longer — your muqriʾ decides the school and the count. Tilmidh shows where madd appears in the Uthmani tajweed text; the mic checks the word identity, not a stopwatch on six ḥarakāt.

ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ

In ar-raḥmān you meet a madd shape that beginners often clip. In Ikhlāṣ, Allāh and aḥad teach you not to invent extra length where there is none, and not to starve length where the text marks it. Recite one word, wait for green, continue.

Honest limit: no app replaces a teacher for madd timing. Tarteel and similar tools try cloud scoring; Tilmidh stays on-device Web Speech and word matching. Use us for fluency and order; use a muqriʾ for ijazah-level length.

Practice path: Ikhlāṣ → Fātiḥah → rule pages for qalqalah and ghunnah. Beginners who pray: best tajwid app for beginners.
