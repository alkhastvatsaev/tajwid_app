  1. Practice qalqalah

    Written by Alkhast Vatsaev · Updated 2026-08-14

    Qalqalah is the slight bounce of a letter when it is sakinah (no vowel) or when you stop on it. Five letters: ق ط ب ج د. Without the bounce the word collapses; with too much bounce you invent a vowel. Tilmidh makes you repeat the word until the mic recognizes it — practice, not a diploma.

    The five letters and where they are articulated

    They are remembered with the phrase quṭb jad (قطب جد). Each one bounces at the exact place where it is pronounced: the bounce is not an added sound, it is the release of the articulation.

    LetterArticulation point (makhraj)
    قback of the tongue against the soft palate
    طtip of the tongue at the base of the upper front teeth
    بboth lips
    جmiddle of the tongue against the palate
    دtip of the tongue at the base of the upper front teeth

    Qalqalah ṣughrā: in the middle of the word

    قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ

    In Qul huwa Allāhu aḥad (Ikhlāṣ 112:1), the ق of qul carries qalqalah ṣughrā: the letter is sakinah in the middle of the word. You do not lengthen. You do not vote an extra u after the qāf. You release the articulation, a short click, then continue huwa.

    Qalqalah kubrā: when you stop on the letter

    Qalqalah kubrā appears when you stop on the letter, for example the د of aḥad in waqf. The bounce is a little more audible, still without an invented vowel. Articulation points stay qāf (uvula), ṭāʾ (tongue tip), bāʾ (lips), jīm (mid-tongue), dāl (dentals).

    The mistake the mic catches most

    Common mic mistake: swallowing the qāf (it becomes kul) or turning it into a hard g. Tilmidh colors tajwid from Quran.com and only advances when the consonant skeleton matches. Recite slowly. If the word is not accepted, repeat that word only.

    Where to go next

    Next, open Falaq (surah 113): another qul, then the qāf of al-falaq if you stop. Alternate Ikhlāṣ and Falaq until the bounce is the same gesture, not a vocal effect. Three short passes a day beat one long weekly session: this is an articulation habit, and habits settle through brief, regular repetition.     Frequently asked questions

    What are the five qalqalah letters?

    ق, ط, ب, ج and د. They are remembered with the phrase quṭb jad (قطب جد). The bounce only happens when the letter is sakinah, or when you stop on it at the end of a word.

    What is the difference between qalqalah ṣughrā and kubrā?

    Ṣughrā applies to a sakinah letter in the middle of a word, like the ق of qul: the bounce is brief. Kubrā applies to a letter you stop on, like the د of aḥad in waqf: the bounce is slightly more audible. Neither adds a vowel.

    Does qalqalah add a vowel after the letter?

    No. That is the most common error. The bounce is the release of the articulation at the same point in the mouth, not a u or e stuck behind the letter. If you hear a clear vowel, the bounce is too strong.
